# How to take a [[screenshot|ref]]
=== ":fontawesome-brands-windows: Windows"
    1. Press ++windows+shift+s++
    2. Draw a rectangular shape with your mouse to capture a specific area.
    3. Click the pop-up window.
    4. Press ++ctrl+c++ to copy.
    5. Open your document and press ++ctrl+v++ to paste.

=== ":fontawesome-brands-apple: macOS"
    1. Press ++cmd+shift+4++
    2. Draw a rectangular shape with your mouse to capture a specific area.
    3. Click the pop-up window.
    4. Press ++cmd+c++ to copy.
    5. Open your document and press ++cmd+v++ to paste.
